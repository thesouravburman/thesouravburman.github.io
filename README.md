## About

I am a third-year Computer Science Engineering student at **Brainware University, Kolkata** (SGPA 8.79) — specialising in AI and Computer Vision. I have built systems that detect eye conditions without any hardware, classify live traffic in real time, read market sentiment from raw text, and a complete personalised AI fitness platform from scratch.

I interned at **Samsung R&D Institute, Kolkata**, trained in **Cryptographic Security at the Indian Statistical Institute** under a MeitY government programme, and completed **Qualcomm's Deep Tech Entrepreneurship** programme with a perfect score.
